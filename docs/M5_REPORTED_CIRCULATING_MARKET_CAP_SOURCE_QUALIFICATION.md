## Basis distinctions and fail-closed rules

Provider-calculated circulating market cap is a distinct basis from a self-reported value. Self-reported supply/value must retain its own classification and cannot satisfy a provider-calculated requirement. `totalSupply x price` is a total/minted-supply-derived value, not circulating market cap. FDV uses maximum supply and is not circulating market cap. Neither may be used as fallback. The scoped contract therefore accepts only an explicitly reported circulating basis; a field label or calculated substitute is insufficient.

CoinGecko's general formula and supply methodology are documented, so the unresolved issue is not absence of a general methodology. It is whether the exact WETH historical value uses that basis, whether supply and quote share the value point's `asOf`, and how the wrapped-token Global Market Cap exclusion applies to this token-address market-data surface.

An endpoint point timestamp does not establish that market cap, circulating supply and quote were measured at the same `asOf`. A missing/null, stale, mismatched or revised value must not project. Historical point availability does not prove completeness or finality.

ETH and WETH are separate representations. Ticker, name, price or peg resemblance creates no mapping authority. A WETH contract address may identify a provider record but does not establish canonical mapping. This change creates no WETH-to-ETH equivalence and no mapping authority.
