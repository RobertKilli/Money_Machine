## Existing M5 contract detail

### Daily close

`m5-daily-series-authority/v1` is scoped to Ethereum mainnet (`eip155:1`) and a lowercase contract address. It binds each positive `bigint` close atom value, a common integer `priceScale` (domain range 0–32767), one `quoteUnit`, canonical UTC millisecond `observedAt`/`availableAt`, `asOf`, unique observation timestamps/IDs and source material fingerprints. The derivation requires at least 15 observations, at least 14 returns, at least 14 whole days of coverage, and rejects gaps over 48 hours, timestamp/observation duplicates, scale/unit mismatch, and future availability. The authority code does **not** enforce that each point is at 00:00 UTC, nor define how a market “close” is computed; the word close is a product/data contract requirement, not proven by the type or a field name. Corrections, venue selection, and longer history horizon are not presently specified by this authority. A successful authority and derived history/volatility remain bound to sealed SourceLineage and all source artifacts, envelopes, observations, external record IDs, and payload fingerprints.

Thus the 15-point/14-day rule and 48h max gap are current implementation policy. Exact UTC boundary, venue/composite selection, a broader history horizon, and correction treatment are unresolved product or source-policy choices; the 48h tolerance does not itself establish daily completeness.

### Market cap

`m5-market-metrics-authority/v1` requires material kind `MARKET_CAP`, basis exactly `MARKET_CAP_REPORTED`, not FDV or an internally calculated value. Inputs are unsigned exact `bigint` atoms up to uint256, scale 0–36, and currency must match the authority's declared quote currency. Normalization uses `MINOR_SCALE_2_FLOOR_V1`: integer minor-unit atoms (result scale 0), flooring source precision beyond two decimals. The authority does not hard-code USD; this decision config currently uses USD. Zero is accepted; null/missing is not a valid numeric material. `observedAt <= availableAt <= asOf` is enforced. Each material retains artifact/envelope/observation/external IDs and payload fingerprint. The authority has no supply-quantity field and no block/finality requirement: those are not current contract requirements. The product meaning of “market cap” (circulating vs total supply) should be explicit before changing basis; currency/as-of/fixed-point and lineage enforcement are existing implementation requirements.

### Volume

The same authority requires kind `VOLUME`, basis exactly `ROLLING_24H_REPORTED`, explicit UTC-millisecond `windowStart` and `windowEnd` separated by exactly 86,400,000 ms, `windowEnd <= asOf`, quote currency matching the authority, and `observedAt <= availableAt <= asOf`. It does not currently require `windowEnd === observedAt` or `windowEnd === asOf`; introducing that binding would be a contract strengthening, not an existing check. The complete market universe, spot/derivative inclusion, DEX coverage, wash/anomaly policy and currency conversion method are not encoded for volume today. CMC's latest `volume_24h` field and aggregate methodology support a present-tense rolling-volume description, but do not prove historical window boundaries or bind them to `asOf`. A fixed UTC daily candle volume is an implementation of a different bucket metric, not this rolling window.

### Authority and persistence

All three metrics flow through versioned authority material and sealed SourceLineage. The market-metrics authority currently also requires a complete versioned liquidity component universe even when only market-cap and volume sourcing is being discussed. Quantitative evidence binds to authority IDs/fingerprints and derivation fingerprints. Persistence is atomic and idempotent; there is no newest-wins repair. Candidate selection cannot waive exact provider/dataset/version, timestamps, source artifacts/envelopes/observations, payload fingerprints, lineage, or existing fail-closed readiness/approval gates.

### Product and implementation boundaries

Product choices: (1) what “daily close” means and which venue/composite it represents; (2) whether WETH may be represented only by WETH-contract markets or whether ETH is ever acceptable (no substitution is assumed); (3) circulating market cap versus total WETH supply value; (4) rolling versus UTC-bucket volume and the included venue/market universe; (5) user-facing redistribution/commercial product scope and data retention needs; (6) recurring budget ceiling.

Implementation choices after those decisions: provider adapter, fixed-point formula and rounding, provider/symbol mapping version, page/range strategy, UTC boundary resolver, finality policy for chain reads, gap/correction handling, fingerprint schema, and SourceLineage members. These cannot silently redefine the product metrics.

## Usage, storage and price are independent

The decision contract records each of the seven existing usage scopes independently—network acquisition, raw-payload processing, raw-payload storage, normalized storage, authority persistence, redistribution, and commercial use—and separately records `retentionApproval`. Retention is not storage permission. Public documentation, a free tier, or a displayed “commercial use” feature is not approval for Money Machine’s intended persistence, derived values, redistribution, retention, or user-facing product. CoinGecko terms restrict copying/storing/deriving without applicable permission and include cache handling requirements; CMC's pricing page advertises commercial use, while its commercial terms limit the license, standalone redistribution, and end-user context—neither statement alone confirms Money Machine's product fit. CoinAPI's guidance says usage may be commercially permitted while its governing legal terms and usage policy still control. A data-retention term cannot grant storage permission, and usage approval cannot be inferred from a plan price. All production readiness and approval config remains unchanged and BLOCKED.

Public price points are time-sensitive estimates only. The amounts above are displayed list prices at review time (USD/month unless stated); tax, currency conversion, quotas, asset coverage, required history, and enterprise terms are excluded. “Unknown” or custom pricing means contact sales / obtain a written quote, not $0.

## Versioned decision model

`config/m5/market-metric-source-gap-decision.production.json` uses `m5-market-metric-source-gap-decision/v1`. Each metric records decision/recommendation, candidate scope, required inputs, a versioned `methodologyId`, methodology, completeness, official evidence references and levels, seven independent usage approvals, independent retention approval, price status, blockers, and review expiry. The parser binds each known candidate to its allowed metric, methodology ID, and evidence-host set. It rejects duplicate metrics/references/usages, unknown or unsafe object/array shapes, accessors, inherited/symbol fields, noncanonical timestamps/URLs, and inconsistent claimed completeness. IDs/fingerprints are deterministic; `recordedAt` is excluded from material identity. Parsed values are deeply frozen. The evaluator is intentionally advisory and always returns `BLOCKED`; it performs no transport, database, persistence, or projection.

The production decisions are all BLOCKED. They do not select a source, grant legal/usage/storage rights, create ingestion, or modify provider readiness. Existing CoinGecko qualification is deliberately not rewritten.
